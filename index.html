ECHOES IN THE DARK

Navigate blind mazes using stereo sonar pings. Wear headphones for left/right audio!

Controls:
• W A S D: Send sonar ping (Up/Left/Down/Right)
• Arrow Keys: Walk 1 step
• H: Use Medkit (+30 HP) | E: Equip Armour (+20%)
• T + WASD: Blast an inner wall, trap, or monster with TNT

Sonar Sounds:
• Pure Tone: Wall (Higher pitch = farther away)
• High Ping (950Hz): Exit
• Double Beep: Spike Trap (-15 HP)
• Low Growl: Monster (Moves after your steps, -25 HP)
• High-Pitched Growl: Monster heard you and is hunting you!
