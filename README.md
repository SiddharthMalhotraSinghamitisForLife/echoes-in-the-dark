Echoes in the Dark is a sound-based maze navigation game with monsters, chests, spike traps and power-ups.

Features:
It has 10 levels.
Sonar scanning to find walls, monsters, exit and traps.
Monsters that move around and then if youa are too close follow you to attack.
Spike traps that disappear once you land in them.
Chests at exit that give medkits, armour plates, tnt or coins depending on rarity of the chest.
In game shop to buy upgrades and items between levels.
Player luck, armour, health that influences damage and other things.
Endless mode with randomly generated levels and progressively bigger and more difficult mazes.

Controls:
WASD - Sonar scan in 4 directions.
Arrow keys - move in any direction.
H - use medkit.
E - use armour plate to increase player armour.
T - arm or disarm tnt.
WASD while tnt is armed - blast tnt in any of the 4 directions.

How to play:
Use the sonar to scan for walls, exit, monsters, traps and take steps moving through the maze following these indicators. Use powerups to help you on difficult levels.
